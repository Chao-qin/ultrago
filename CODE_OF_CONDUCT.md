# Code of Conduct

UltraGo is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code, and respect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content and may restrict participation when needed. Report concerns privately through the repository's security contact or GitHub's reporting tools.
